Agent Multi Tab Use

Let every AI agent on your PC work in your Chrome at the same time, each in its own tab group.

  1. Download the companion app

    Free and small. Agents connect to it, and it passes their commands to this extension.

    Download for Windows

  2. Open AgentMultiTabUse.exe

    It runs quietly in the background and starts with Windows. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

    Waiting for the app

  3. Hand your agents their setup

    Create tab groups, then copy the ready-made instructions into Claude, Codex, Hermes or any agent.

    Open dashboard

Everything stays on this PC: the app only listens on 127.0.0.1 and sends nothing online.
Chrome shows a "started debugging this browser" bar on tabs agents control. That's expected.
